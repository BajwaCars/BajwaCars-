## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-trust, prestigious automotive experience tailored for the Pakistani premium vehicle market, bridging luxury Japanese domestic market (JDM) imports, certified pre-owned domestic vehicles, and executive rental fleets.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The aesthetic balances **Precision Modernism** with an **Executive Automotive Showroom** presence:
- Architectural density: Crisp alignments, high-contrast structural dividers, and deliberate whitespace framing automotive photography.
- High-clarity verification: Trust signals (PakWheels Inspection, JDM Auction Grade Sheets, Verified Titles) are integrated as structural, high-visibility badges rather than decorative afterthoughts.
- Localized financial prestige: Typography and layouts natively accommodate Pakistani rupee conventions (PKR, Lakhs, Crores) with numeric prominence and visual dignity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
- **Audience:** High-net-worth individuals, overseas Pakistanis sourcing verified imports, business executives requiring chauffeured rentals, and discerning automotive enthusiasts seeking immaculate JDM machinery.
- **Emotional Response:** Absolute transparency, structural reliability, mechanical precision, and prestige without ostentation.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored in deep naval tones and automotive metallics, ignited by warm amber-gold accents that convey executive prestige and energetic momentum.

### Roles & Palette Composition
- **Primary (`#0F2D59`)**: Royal Navy Blue. Represents institutional authority, corporate governance, and primary visual anchors (headers, primary CTAs, active states).
- **Secondary (`#D97706`) / Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: High-visibility energy used selectively for conversion triggers, featured badges, price callouts, and rating stars.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`) / Verified Teal**: Used strictly for authentication indicators: "JDM Auction Verified", "PakWheels Inspected", and 100% verified documentation checkmarks.
- **Deep Slate Neutral (`#0A192F`)**: Ink Navy for primary typography, dark mode surfaces, and footer scaffolding.
- **Metallic Slate & Surface Neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`)**: Crisp light backgrounds emulating pristine showroom floors, chrome trims, and subtle divider lines.

### Color Accessibility & Theme Strategy
- Primary CTAs (`#0F2D59`) leverage pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), exceeding WCAG AAA contrast ratio (11.2:1).
- Amber highlight accents use `#D97706` for text against light backgrounds to guarantee readability (4.6:1), while `#F59E0B` is reserved for filled badge surfaces with dark ink text.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Outfit** for structural geometry, bold numerals, and authoritative display titles with **Inter** for dense vehicular specifications, inspection reports, and rental policy copy.

### Architectural Rules
- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Modern geometric grotesk with tight apertures and clean line terminals. Used for vehicle model designations (e.g., *Land Cruiser ZX 3.3D*, *Civic RS Turbo*), pricing, and promotional banners.
- **Currency & Localized Numerics (Outfit):** Price blocks must display Pakistani currency designations with visual priority: the numerical amount renders in `700` weight (e.g., `1.85`), followed by localized units (`Crore`, `Lacs`, or `PKR`) in uppercase `600` weight at 80% scale.
- **Data & Readout Specs (Inter):** Milage (`km`), Engine displacement (`cc`), Model Year, and Auction Grades render in Inter to preserve tabular legibility in high-density data matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt structural rhythm informs both screen layouts and vehicular card grids.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, maximum container boundary of `80rem` (1280px) centered with dynamic margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters, accommodating 2-column inventory card grids.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and edge-to-edge horizontal carousels for filter chips and trending luxury inventory.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Tight vertical coupling (`0.25rem` - `0.5rem`) binds vehicle year, make, and trim.
- Medium separation (`1rem` - `1.5rem`) isolates interactive controls, spec pills, and booking forms.
- Generous macro-spacing (`3rem` - `4rem`) divides vehicle showcases, rental terms, and dealership showroom contact blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **metallic layered surfaces** and **cold-tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding muddy drop shadows to emulate precision-polished vehicle paint and glass.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Showroom Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base surface for main page canvas.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` for automotive vehicle cards, modal dialogues, and inspection tabs.
- **Tier 2 (Raised Controls):** `#FFFFFF` with outline `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays & Rental Bottomsheets):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.12)`.
- **Accent Elevation (Active / Highlighted Cards):** Subtle blue-tinted rim glow using `0 0 0 1px #0F2D59, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 45, 89, 0.15)`.

### Border Rationale
To maintain high-precision mechanical cues, card elements and inputs do not rely solely on shadow for boundary definition; they feature crisp `1px` borders in `#E2E8F0`, shifting to `#CBD5E1` on hover.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft Geometric (`roundedness: 1`)** architecture:
- Base components (cards, standard buttons, text fields) feature `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corners.
- Badges, pill tags, and quick-filter switches use semi-pill or full pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately separate categorical triggers from structural content cards.
- Image containers maintain `0.25rem` corners with precise clipping to preserve vehicle body lines and wheel arches.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Now / Test Drive / Reserve Rental):** Solid Royal Navy (`#0F2D59`) background, pure white Outfit Bold text, subtle inset top border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`). Hover transforms to `#0A192F` with a slight 1px elevation lift.
- **Accent Conversion (Call Dealership / WhatsApp / Instant Offer):** Solid Warm Amber (`#D97706`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Used for high-conversion dealership leads.
- **Secondary / Ghost (View Inspection Report / Full Specs):** Transparent fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F2D59`. Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9`.

### 2. Trust Badges & Verification Tags
- **PakWheels Inspected:** Light neutral slate pill (`#F1F5F9`), `1px solid #CBD5E1`, leading green check icon (`#0D9488`), dark navy text, and score block (e.g., `9.2/10`).
- **JDM Auction Sheet Verified:** High-contrast badge with teal accent tint (`#F0FDFA`), border `#99F6E4`, text `#0F766E`, showing auction grade (e.g., `Grade 4.5 / A`).
- **Karachi Registered / Unregistered:** Solid deep navy tag (`#0A192F`) with crisp white micro-type (`11px`, bold, uppercase).

### 3. Filter Chips & Quick Selectors
- **Unselected:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.
- **Selected:** Background `#0F2D59`, border `1px solid #0F2D59`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Categories: Body Type (Sedan, SUV, Crossover, Luxury), Transmission, Fuel (Hybrid, Petrol, EV), Price Range (Under 50 Lacs, 50-1 Crore, 1-3 Crore, 3 Crore+).

### 4. Vehicle Inventory Cards
- **Structure:** 16:10 aspect ratio vehicle photography container, overlaid with verification pills at top-left and heart/bookmark button at top-right.
- **Card Body:**
  - Sub-label: Model Year & Assembly origin (e.g., *2022 • Japanese Import*).
  - Main Title: Make & Model in Outfit Bold (`18px`).
  - Spec Matrix: 3-column micro-grid with dividers: Mileage (e.g., `24,000 km`), Engine (e.g., `1798 cc`), Fuel/Transmission (e.g., `Hybrid Auto`).
  - Footer Anchor: Localized PKR price display aligned left, secondary CTA button right-aligned.

### 5. Rental Fleet Specific Cards
- Similar baseline structure to inventory cards, featuring dual pricing readouts: "PKR 25,000 / Day (With Driver)" and "PKR 18,000 / Day (Self-Drive)".

### 6. Input Fields & Form Elements
- Inputs feature crisp `#F8FAFC` background with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, transitioning to `#0F2D59` with a `2px` focus ring in `rgba(15, 45, 89, 0.15)`.
- Integrated PKR prefix indicator locked on the left inside financial calculation and bidding inputs.
